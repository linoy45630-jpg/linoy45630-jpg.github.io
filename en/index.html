About me

I'm Linoy, a third-year student of Learning Technologies at Holon Institute of Technology (HIT).

My path to learning design started in the field. As a command and control systems instructor at the IDF Command and Control School and in the Golani Brigade, I trained officers and soldiers on command and control systems, led the rollout of a combat planning system, and was responsible for certifying around 500 soldiers. At Elbit Systems I implemented defense systems in the field and built training content, working directly with clients and end users.

In the reserves, I set up my unit's operations room from scratch, along with its knowledge management. At the start of the war and throughout it, I built training and content and certified the operations room staff. Today I also design and mentor command post exercises at Amarel.

I see the client, listen to the learner, and use technology to build the precise solution.

My degree added a wide technology toolkit to that experience: e-learning, Unity learning games, video, UX design, research and AI. I love the moment when precise content meets a technical solution that truly works for the people learning.

What makes me different

I see the field and the end user

Years of training and implementation taught me what a learning moment looks like in the real world. So before I start building, I make sure the idea will truly serve the people who use it.

Deep, precise analysis

I don't stop at the first request. I dig into the need, rely on what people actually say and do, and build a solution that fits its audience.

Advanced technology, learning on the move

Working with advanced software and AI tools from different fields is part of my everyday. I keep learning and grow with every new development in technology.
